How this works
  1. Doves don't leave on a schedule — they leave when a cold front pushes through and gives them a north wind to ride.
  2. So we watch four stretches of country north of your fields: central Oklahoma, southern Kansas, central Kansas, and Nebraska.
  3. When a front crosses one of them, the birds staged there take off with it. The farther north they were, the longer their flight — roughly a day of flying per 150 miles.
  4. The front moves faster than the birds do. So the cool weather gets to you first, and the birds show up a day or two behind it. That gap is what this whole thing is for.
